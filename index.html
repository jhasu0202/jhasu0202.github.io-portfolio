Citrus Disease Detection

Problem:
Classify citrus leaf diseases across visually similar categories.

Dataset:
2027 images (1808 train / 219 test) with real-world variability.

Approach:
HSV + GLCM + LBP → Random Forest

Result:
92% accuracy on unseen test data

Why this works:
Performs better than CNNs on small, structured datasets.

Limitations:
Sensitive to extreme lighting and unseen classes.
